Zones

Save

Save as weights/zones.json in the repository; the pipeline (src/roadwatch/scene.py) reads it next to weights/scene.json.

Coordinates are normalised to [0, 1] of the image width and height (x right, y down). A stop line’s dir is the travel direction of approaching traffic in degrees, atan2(dy, dx) with y down. Work is autosaved in this browser; the background image is not.
