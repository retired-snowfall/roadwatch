Video annotator

Label the sample videos to build a dev set in the official ground-truth format. Videos play from your disk and are never uploaded; labels are saved in this browser as you work.

Drop .mp4 files here or click to choose. You can open several; labels are kept per file name.

Ground truth

Import merges into the current labels. Export covers every video in this browser, keyed by file name:

{"sample_001.mp4": {
  "duration": 60.0, "fps": 25.0,
  "events": [[5.0, 8.0, "red_light"],

Compare with predictions

A predictions JSON ({"team": …, "videos": {name: {"events": [[s, e, label], …]}}}) is shown as faded bars in a “pred” row under each class. Read-only.

Labelling conventions
ClassStartEnd
accidentfirst frame where contact is visibleall involved objects stop moving or leave the frame
near_missonset of the evasive actionroad users are clear of each other
red_lightfront of the vehicle crosses the stop line (signal red)vehicle leaves the intersection or the frame
wrong_wayvehicle enters the opposing lanereturns to a correct lane or leaves the frame
illegal_u_turnvehicle starts turningcompletes the turn
stopped_vehiclevehicle stops (stationary ≥ 10 s on the carriageway, not queued at a signal)moves again or is removed
jaywalkingpedestrian steps onto the road outside a crossingleaves the road
failure_to_yieldvehicle enters the crossing while a pedestrian is on itleaves the crossing
illegal_turnvehicle starts turningcompletes the turn
solid_line_crossingwheel crosses the solid linevehicle fully in the new lane
stop_linevehicle stops past the stop line on redsignal turns green
congestionqueue stops moving (standstill/crawl across all lanes of a direction)queue clears
road_obstacleobstacle appearsobstacle is removed
fire_smokefirst visible smokesmoke clears or the frame ends

When an event runs past the end of the video, end = video duration. Two simultaneous events of the same class are one segment.
