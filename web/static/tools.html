Tools

Two small in-browser tools we use to measure and tune the detector. Nothing is uploaded: videos and images stay on your machine.

Annotate a video

Build a dev set in the official ground-truth format.

  • Open local sample videos, pick one of the 14 classes, mark start and end with S / E.
  • Swimlane timeline, overlap and range checks, predictions overlay for comparison.
  • Exports {"sample_001.mp4": {"duration", "fps", "events"}}.
Open the annotator

Scene editor

Draw crosswalks, stop lines, solid lines and no-turn rules for the fixed camera.

  • Draw on a background frame; coordinates are stored normalised to [0, 1].
  • Stop lines carry the travel direction of the approaching traffic.
  • Exports weights/zones.json, read by the pipeline.
Open the scene editor

Why these exist

The organisers score submissions on a hidden, labelled test set, so the only way to measure F1 before submitting is to label the sample videos ourselves in exactly their format. The annotator produces that dev set, and the evaluation script compares our predictions against it. The camera is fixed, so the road layout — crossings, stop lines, solid lines, prohibited turns — only has to be drawn once and is reused for every video.
